Agent-manager comparison

A side-by-side of AI coding-agent managers: tools that supervise a fleet of coding-agent sessions (Claude Code, Codex, and similar) across usage tracking, task sourcing, session organization, and remote/mobile access. Built to find where a personal, terminal-native project genuinely differs from the field, not as marketing for any one tool.

Snapshot as of 2026-09-28. Cells come from each tool's own docs, sites, or posts at the dates shown for that tool, not re-verified beyond that pass. Where a tool's own materials don't state something, the cell reads "unknown" rather than a guess.
Compiled by Daniel Mack (dtiger1889-ops). Pantheon is the author's own project and is scored by the same rules as everything else here.
